My dream AI startup

AI can apply to almost every aspect of human life to greatly improve efficiency. One area that need most attention is the traditional legal/law apartment which still depends on systems created 20 years ago. The evidence collection system, handling system as well as investigation analysis are still very manual which is not able to keep up with the increasing demand on justice. Thus, I am looking to combine latest AI technologies to digitalize this whole system to make it much more efficient.
